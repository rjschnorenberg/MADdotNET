# AI, Developer Tools & Automation

## Graphify
An open-source tool and AI assistant skill that parses codebases, documentation, SQL schemas, and media into queryable knowledge graphs. It allows AI coding agents (such as Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex) to navigate software architecture structurally rather than relying solely on file search/grep.
  * [https://graphify.net/](https://graphify.net/)

## Instacart MCP (Model Context Protocol)
An integration built on Anthropic’s open-standard Model Context Protocol (MCP) that allows AI agents and LLMs to search for groceries, manage digital shopping carts, and automate ordering on Instacart.
  * [https://docs.instacart.com/developer_platform_api/guide/tutorials/mcp](https://docs.instacart.com/developer_platform_api/guide/tutorials/mcp)

## Microsoft / Puppeteer & Playwright
Headless browser automation and end-to-end testing tools. Meetup discussions typically center on Google’s Puppeteer versus Microsoft’s Playwright (built by former Puppeteer engineers) for scraping, testing, and AI-driven web navigation.
  * [https://playwright.dev/](https://playwright.dev/)
  * [https://pptr.dev/](https://pptr.dev/)

# Home Infrastructure & Water Treatment

## Water Softeners
Systems used to remove calcium, magnesium, and mineral hardness from municipal or well water—a frequent topic among Midwest homeowners.
  
### Fleck
Industry-standard control valves and water softening units manufactured under the Pentair brand, known for high reliability and DIY serviceability.
  * [https://www.pentair.com/](https://www.pentair.com/)
### Capital Water Softener
A Madison/McFarland, Wisconsin-based water conditioning company and manufacturer known locally for residential and commercial water treatment equipment.
  * [https://www.capwater.com/](https://www.capwater.com/)
