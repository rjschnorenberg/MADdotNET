## 1. AI Coding Agents, Workflows & Developer Tools

* **[AgentsView](https://www.agentsview.io/)**  
  A local-first analytics dashboard and session viewer that automatically detects and aggregates local logs from over 60 AI coding agents (including Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and Gemini) into a searchable archive with activity tracking and API token cost analysis.
* **[Parallel Works: Plans, Goals & Checkpoints (`pw code`)](https://parallelworks.com/docs/ai/code/plan-and-goals)**  
  Documentation for the `pw code` AI coding tool detailing structured agentic workflows, including a read-only Plan Mode (`/plan`), condition-driven autonomous execution loops (`/goal`), recurring scheduled background prompts (`/loop`), and session state checkpoints (`/rewind`).
* **[Work Louder: Codex Micro](https://worklouder.cc/codex-micro)**  
  A compact, 13-key mechanical RGB macropad created in collaboration with OpenAI that integrates directly with the ChatGPT/Codex desktop app, featuring status-lit Agent Keys, a planar joystick, and a rotary dial for tactile management of concurrent AI agent threads and push-to-talk voice prompts.
* **[Hyperoptimized Engineer 2026 (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MhEL9cvLxQE)**  
  A satirical comedy sketch by Kai Lentit spoofing ultra-optimized 2026 software engineers who abandon typing in favor of Stream Deck git triggers, run 16 concurrent coding agents in detached worktrees overnight, and automate their entire personal lives and company meetings.

## 2. Applied AI, Industry Trends & Everyday Use Cases

* **[PrismScope](https://prismscope.ai/)**  
  An AI-powered conversational survey and interview platform that replaces static 1–5 rating scales with adaptive follow-up questions to uncover the underlying "why" behind customer, product, and multi-stakeholder event feedback, backed by verifiable source transcripts.
* **[Business Insider: Fake AI Job Candidates Pass Interviews and Vanish, Arena CEO Says](https://www.businessinsider.com/fake-ai-job-candidates-pass-interviews-vanish-arena-ceo-2026-8)**  
  An article examining warnings from Arena co-founder and CEO Anastasios Angelopoulos regarding "vaporware" job applicants—autonomous or AI-assisted fake candidates capable of passing rigorous technical engineering interviews before vanishing during hiring verification.
* **[Coursiv Blog: AI Crochet Patterns — What to Know Before You Stitch](https://coursiv.io/blog/ai-crochet-patterns)**  
  A practical guide exploring how chatbots and pattern generators frequently hallucinate stitch counts and shaping geometry in crochet instructions, showing crafters how to spot unverified AI patterns while safely using AI for gauge math and terminology translation.

## 3. Hardware, Spatial Computing & Peripherals

* **[XREAL AURA](https://www.xreal.com/us/aura)**  
  Wired optical see-through AR glasses paired with a Snapdragon-powered portable computing puck running Android XR, featuring dual 120Hz Sony Micro-OLED displays, a 70-degree field of view, 6DoF spatial anchoring, electrochromic dimming, and built-in multimodal Google Gemini AI integration.
* **[OpenMouse Control Panel](https://control.openmouse.app/)**  
  A free, open-source, browser-based mouse configurator that uses WebHID to adjust DPI, polling rates, button mappings, and RGB lighting across dozens of gaming mice directly in Chromium-based browsers without installing proprietary vendor software.

## 6. Psychology, Personality & Workplace Dynamics

* **[Quiet: The Power of Introverts in a World That Can't Stop Talking (Goodreads)](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/8520610-quiet)**  
  Susan Cain’s bestselling nonfiction book examining how modern culture and workplaces overvalue the "Extrovert Ideal" while highlighting the vital strengths introverts bring to deep problem-solving, creativity, and leadership.
* **[Cleveland Clinic: Omnivert vs. Ambivert — What's the Difference?](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/omnivert-vs-ambivert)**  
  A psychology explainer contrasting ambiverts—who maintain a steady, moderate balance between introversion and extroversion—with omniverts, who swing between the far extremes of high-energy socializing and complete solitude depending on their immediate internal needs.
