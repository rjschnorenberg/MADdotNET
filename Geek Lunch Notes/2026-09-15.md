## 1. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

* **[DeepSWE (Datacurve)](https://deepswe.datacurve.ai/)**  
  A software engineering evaluation benchmark designed to assess frontier coding agents on original, complex, multi-file engineering tasks in isolated environments, serving as a more robust alternative to SWE-bench.
* **[Hermes Agent (Nous Research)](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/)**  
  An open-source, autonomous AI agent capable of persistent memory across sessions, dynamic self-directed skill learning, subagent delegation, and cross-platform communication (CLI, Discord, Telegram, Slack).
* **[Ollama Cloud Models](https://ollama.com/search?c=cloud)**  
  The search and discovery directory for cloud-compatible models supported in the Ollama ecosystem, allowing developers to pull and interface with remote LLMs alongside local deployments.
* **[OpenAI Daybreak](https://openai.com/daybreak/)**  
  OpenAI’s enterprise cyber-defense stack and initiative that uses agentic models and the Codex harness to automate vulnerability discovery, validation, patching, and remediation workflows for defenders.
* **[Science: How an AI Math Breakthrough Ignited Controversy](https://www.science.org/content/article/how-ai-math-breakthrough-ignited-controversy)**  
  An investigative piece covering academic controversies and debates surrounding recent AI models claiming breakthrough solutions to complex mathematical conjectures, raising questions about attribution, proof verifiability, and true mathematical comprehension.

## 3. Home Networking, DNS & Security

* **[Ubiquiti UniFi Dream Router 7 (UDR7)](https://store.ui.com/us/en/products/udr7)**  
  Ubiquiti’s all-in-one desktop cloud gateway featuring integrated Wi-Fi 7, multi-gigabit/10G connectivity, built-in PoE switching, and complete support for the UniFi OS application suite.
* **[Pi-hole](https://pi-hole.net/)**  
  An open-source DNS sinkhole and network-wide tracker/ad-blocker designed to run on local hardware (like a Raspberry Pi or home server) to protect all connected network devices without client software.
* **[Cloudflare 1.1.1.3 (DNS for Families)](https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-1-1-1-1-for-families/)**  
  Cloudflare’s security-focused public resolver address configured specifically to block both known malware domains and adult content at the DNS resolution layer.
* **[GRC’s DNS Benchmark](https://www.grc.com/dns/benchmark.htm)**  
  Steve Gibson’s classic, lightweight Windows utility used to benchmark, graph, and compare the operational speed and reliability of local, ISP, and public DNS resolvers.

## 4. Retrocomputing & Vintage Hardware

* **[Vintage Computer Festival Midwest (VCF Midwest)](https://vcfmw.org/)**  
  A prominent annual community festival and exhibition held near Chicago (Schaumburg, IL) celebrating computing history, retro microcomputers, homebrew hardware, and electronics tinkering.
* **[Commodore 64 Ultimate (Ultimate 64)](https://ultimate64.com/)**  
  A modern FPGA-based hardware implementation and enhancement ecosystem for the Commodore 64, providing cycle-accurate emulation, HDMI video output, USB storage, Ethernet connectivity, and dual SID sound chip support.

## 6. Books, Media & Cultural Commentary

* **[Little Eyes (Novel by Samanta Schweblin)](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/57004891-little-eyes)**  
  A speculative fiction novel exploring surveillance, loneliness, and human voyeurism through "kentukis"—small, wheeled toy pets containing cameras controlled anonymously by strangers around the globe.
* **[You Can See Everything (Film)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything)**  
  A documentary film directed by Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim documenting former Theranos CEO Elizabeth Holmes and partner Billy Evans in the final weeks before Holmes began serving her federal prison sentence.
* **[The Singularity Is Near (Book by Ray Kurzweil)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Singularity_Is_Near)**  
  Ray Kurzweil’s landmark 2005 work examining exponential technological progress, the law of accelerating returns, and the forecasted convergence of human biology and artificial superintelligence.
* **[Dr. Strangelove (Film by Stanley Kubrick)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Strangelove)**  
  Stanley Kubrick’s satirical 1964 cold-war masterpiece about nuclear deterrence, human frailty, and the fatal risks of irreversible, automated "Doomsday" fail-safes.
* **[Wildwood (Novel by Colin Meloy)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildwood_(novel))**  
  A bestselling middle-grade fantasy adventure written by The Decemberists’ frontman Colin Meloy and illustrated by Carson Ellis, set in a hidden, enchanted wilderness adjacent to Portland, Oregon.
* **[Rest of World: China Robot Ban & Silicon Valley](https://restofworld.org/2026/china-robot-ban-silicon-valley/)**  
  An article detailing the growing geopolitical and regulatory tensions in Silicon Valley around export controls, supply chain constraints, and US restrictions on Chinese robotics and automation hardware.
